## 6. Key Projects / Case Studies

### Multi-Branch Bookkeeping System — RBJ Seat Master Philippines Co.
- **Challenge:** A 4-branch automotive accessories business with no centralized bookkeeping system, creating risk around BIR compliance, payroll accuracy, and financial visibility across branches.
- **Solution:** Designed a complete bookkeeping infrastructure built around Google Sheets, including a payroll dashboard driven by live data.
- **Implementation:** Built deduction logic covering the full Philippine statutory schedule — SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG, and withholding tax — so payroll calculates correctly across all 4 branches without manual recalculation.
- **Result:** Centralized, BIR-aligned bookkeeping and payroll infrastructure covering 4 branches in the GMA region.
