# Executive Profile — Cherrylyn Alcaraz

*(Two-page narrative biography for the portfolio's About/Cover section)*

## Page One

Cherrylyn Alcaraz built CHEM Tourist and Transport Services OPC around a simple observation: corporate clients in the Philippines don't struggle to find transport vendors — they struggle to find ones they can actually rely on. Inconsistent dispatch, undocumented driver performance, and vendors who treat every booking as a one-off transaction are the norm in the sector. Cherrylyn set out to build the opposite — a transport operation run with the same accountability and systems discipline a corporate client would expect from any other vendor on their books.

As Founder and CEO, Cherrylyn owns every layer of the business: financial decision-making, procurement, client relationships, fleet operations, and driver workforce management. That range is by necessity rather than preference — running a transport company at this stage means functioning as CFO, operations director, head of business development, and HR lead simultaneously. It has also made her fluent across the full operating stack of the business, from the numbers behind a pricing decision to the day-to-day realities of managing a driver workforce.
