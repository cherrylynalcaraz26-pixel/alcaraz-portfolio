# Executive Profile — Cherrylyn Alcaraz

*(Two-page narrative biography for the portfolio's About/Cover section)*

## Page One

Cherrylyn Alcaraz does not run one business. She runs five relationships with five businesses at once — as owner, as manager, and as the consultant other business owners call when their books, their operations, and their systems have outgrown improvisation — and has done so for more than nine years without any of them slipping into disorder.

Her primary role is Founder and President of CHEM Tourist and Transport Services OPC, a corporate B2B shuttle and fleet company she built to serve manufacturing and industrial clients across the CALABARZON region. The business runs more than 16 vehicles — vans, L300 units, and SUVs — under long-term contracts that move hundreds of employees to and from work every day across multiple routes. Cherrylyn owns every functional layer of that operation herself: she negotiates the corporate contracts, designs the routes, deploys and manages the drivers, runs billing and collections, administers payroll and statutory benefits, and carries the full compliance load of LTO, LTFRB, and BIR documentation. That compliance record has stayed clean — zero major violations — for the entire time she has run the company, a detail that matters more in this industry than almost any other claim she could make.

Cherrylyn treats operational visibility as something you build, not something you hope for. Rather than adopting off-the-shelf software, she designed and built the CHEM Transport Management System herself — a platform covering dispatch, trip and fuel monitoring, maintenance scheduling, payroll, and role-based access for drivers, office staff, and management. She has also restructured the company's scheduling and dispatch logic to cut overtime and idle-vehicle time, and is currently leading the transition of the fleet from older L300 units to modern van configurations. The most recent proof point of that discipline is a Q1 CY2026 operational performance report built to presentation grade for corporate clients — the kind of reporting most transport operators her size don't produce at all.

Transport is not where her operating range stops. Cherrylyn also manages Chef David Catering Services, a food-service concession that has run continuously inside an industrial park since 2013 — over a decade of keeping a food-service operation compliant and functioning inside a demanding institutional environment. She additionally manages CDSC Industrial Supply, a B2B supplier of office, facility, and industrial goods to government agencies, hospitals, and industrial companies.

## Page Two

Since 2024, Cherrylyn has turned the same systems discipline into a formal consulting practice, providing bookkeeping and BIR compliance support to other SME owners. Her most substantial engagement to date is RBJ Seat Master Philippines Co., a multi-branch automotive accessories business, where she designed the complete bookkeeping infrastructure across four branches in the GMA region — including a payroll dashboard that pulls live data from Google Sheets and applies the full Philippine statutory deduction schedule (SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG, and withholding tax) automatically — and built the RBJ ERP System, a multi-branch platform covering inventory, point-of-sale, accounting, manufacturing coordination, and supplier management with a consolidated analytics dashboard. For CDSC, she built a consignment reconciliation system that surfaces billing gaps and collection discrepancies before they become disputes.

In 2025 she formalized a second consulting practice — Procurement & ERP Systems Consultant through BlueScale Integrated Business Support — supporting business owners with strategic sourcing, supplier evaluation, and purchase planning, and building the BlueScale Procurement Platform: a supplier portal with quotation management, vendor database, and purchase order tracking. Across her own businesses and her client work, she has now designed and built four distinct systems — a transport management platform, a multi-branch ERP, a procurement platform, and a cross-business executive dashboard consolidating financial and operational KPIs across her full portfolio.

Cherrylyn's operating philosophy is straightforward: a business that depends entirely on the owner's memory is not actually a business yet, it's a habit. Her instinct in every venture she touches is to convert what's informal into what's documented — and increasingly, into what's engineered: a route into a schedule, a handshake agreement into a contract, a shoebox of receipts into a reconciled ledger, a spreadsheet into a system. That instinct is reinforced by her technical range: she builds in React, Next.js, and Supabase, works comfortably with SQL and REST APIs, and holds working knowledge of BIR tax compliance including loose-leaf books, VAT, and EWT. She is currently pursuing Xero Advisor certification.

Her background is technical-vocational, from City College of Laguna — a foundation that shows in how she works: less interested in theory than in whether a system holds up under real operational load, five businesses and four engineered systems deep. That practical instinct is what has let her scale from a single transport contract to a multi-entity operating portfolio without losing control of the compliance, financial, and technical detail that operators at her scale usually delegate away, and it's the same instinct she now brings to every SME client and business owner she works with.
